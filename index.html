THE LAST MESSAGE 📩

A Thriller by Umer Farooq

Chapter 1 — 2:13 AM 🕒

The clock on Umer's phone changed to 2:13 AM. He was still awake. His room was completely quiet, save for the slow, rhythmic hum of the ceiling fan above him.

Then, his phone vibrated. 📱

Unknown Number: Don't open your bedroom door.

Umer frowned. For a few seconds, he assumed it was just a bad joke. Then, another message arrived:

Unknown Number: Someone is standing outside.

Umer's eyes shifted toward his bedroom door. Beneath it, a thin line of darkness appeared. A shadow was blocking the light. Someone was standing right on the other side.

He stopped breathing. He didn't move. He didn't make a sound. 🤫

His phone vibrated again—this time, an incoming call from Alia. Umer answered quietly.

"Alia?"

Her voice sounded nervous. "Umer, where are you?"

"In my room. Why?"

"Listen to me carefully. Don't open your door."

Umer stared at the shadow. "How do you know someone is outside?"

Alia went silent.

"Alia?"

Finally, she whispered: "Because I'm outside."

Umer's face went pale. "You're outside my room?"

"Yes."

"Why?"

"I can't explain right now. Just don't open it."

Umer took a step backward as his phone vibrated again with another text from the Unknown Number:

Unknown Number: She's lying.

"Alia... who is sending me these messages?" Umer asked into the phone.

"I don't know."

"You're hiding something."

"I know," her voice trailed off into a quiet whisper. "I'm sorry."

Suddenly, the shadow under the door vanished. Umer waited, but nothing happened. Slowly, he walked toward the door.

"Umer, don't!"

Ignoring her warning, he reached for the handle and opened it. 🚪

The hallway was empty. Alia wasn't there. Umer looked left, then right—nothing. But as he stepped out, he noticed a small, yellowed envelope lying on the floor with his name written across it: UMER.

He opened it. Inside was an old photograph of him and Alia standing in front of a house he had never seen before. He flipped it over. On the back, a date was written: August 30, 2018. Beneath the date, someone had scribbled:

You promised you would never come back.

His phone vibrated one more time.

Unknown Number: Ask Alia about the basement.

Chapter 2 — The House 🏚️

The next morning, Umer went straight to Alia's house. She was waiting near the gate. The moment she saw the photograph in his hand, her expression turned cold.

"You found it," she said.

"Do you know this picture?" Umer asked. "Where was it taken?"

"At the old house," she replied slowly. "The one we used to visit."

"I've never seen that place in my life."

Alia looked confused. "You really don't remember?"

"No."

She took a slow breath. "Then maybe we should go there."

That afternoon, they traveled to the old house. It stood far from the main road, surrounded by tall trees and crumbling stone walls. Its windows were covered in thick dust, and the front door was standing wide open. 🌲

They stepped inside. The air was frigid, and the house was stripped of furniture. On the wall hung an old clock, its hands frozen at 2:13.

Before Umer could process the coincidence, a loud BANG echoed from downstairs. Both of them froze.

"Someone is down there," Umer said, moving toward the basement door.

"Don't go," Alia begged, grabbing his arm. But Umer pushed forward, descending into the pitch-black basement.

Using his phone flashlight, Umer navigated through boxes, old newspapers, and broken furniture. Scattered across the floor were dozens of photographs of him and Alia as children.

Then, he found a photograph of Alia standing alone. On the back, the same date was written: August 30, 2018. The night Umer disappeared.

Umer slowly turned to her. "What does this mean? Alia, I was here? What happened to me?"

"You don't remember because you weren't supposed to," she replied softly.

Suddenly, heavy footsteps echoed on the floorboards directly above them. Someone was walking through the house. 👥

Umer instantly switched off his flashlight. They stood completely still in the dark as the footsteps stopped above them, followed by the sound of a closing door.

Chapter 3 — The Truth 🔍

When the house finally fell silent, they crept upstairs, only to find the front door locked from the outside.

Searching for another exit behind a cabinet, Umer discovered a narrow door leading to a hidden room. Inside sat an old desk, a metal key, and a cassette recorder with a note: For Umer.

Umer pressed play. Static filled the room, followed by his own voice:

"Alia, if you're hearing this, something went wrong. If I forget everything, don't tell me the truth immediately. Someone is watching us. We found something in the basement, and they don't want anyone to know about it..."

Behind a hidden panel in the room, Umer found a newspaper clipping dated August 30, 2018. The headline read: LOCAL MAN MISSING AFTER FIRE — FAMILY CLAIMS HE RAN AWAY.

"This was the night I lost my memory," Umer realized aloud. "Why didn't you tell me?"

"Because someone warned me not to," Alia said.

Before Umer could ask more, his phone buzzed three times in rapid succession:

Unknown Number: You were never supposed to find the room.
Unknown Number: Leave the house.
Unknown Number: Before she tells you everything.

Umer turned to Alia. "What haven't you told me?"

Tears filled her eyes as she took the photograph from his hand and pointed to a line of text he had missed earlier:

I wasn't the one who disappeared.

Alia looked up at him. "It means you were trying to find me."

Chapter 4 — The Last Message 👁️

Flashes of forgotten memories suddenly rushed back to Umer—a dark hallway, a broken window, Alia shouting his name, and a mysterious man standing behind them saying: "Forget this place."

The messages weren't trying to haunt him. They were trying to make him remember.

Umer checked the Unknown sender details on his phone. There was no phone number, no IP address, no name—just Unknown.

Then, one final message popped up:

Unknown Number: Look behind you.

Umer slowly turned around. Nobody was standing there, but pinned to the wall was a fresh photograph that hadn't been there before.

It showed him and Alia in 2018, but standing right behind them was a tall man whose face had been aggressively scratched out. On the back, a sentence was written:

He is the reason you forgot.

"Do you know who he is?" Umer whispered.

Alia stared at the scratched-out face, her expression turning to pure horror. "Yes... my father. But he was supposed to be dead."

Suddenly, the lights flickered and went out completely. From the floor above them, three loud knocks echoed: KNOCK. KNOCK. KNOCK. 🚪💥

Umer's phone screen lit up in the dark with one last message:

Unknown Number: I'm not dead.
